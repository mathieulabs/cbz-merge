<h1 align="center">CBZ Merge</h1>

<p align="center">Merge manga and webtoon chapters (<code>.cbz</code>) into bigger files, right on your phone.</p>

- Pick a folder or files, choose a cover, split by pages or seasons
- Optional compression to get smaller files

## Install

Download the APK from the [Releases](../../releases) page. Android 8.0 or newer.

## Build

Open in Android Studio and press Run.
